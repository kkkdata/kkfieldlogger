这套系统解决什么What it's for

这不是相册，是证据库。 Not a photo album. An evidence system.

为需要"事后说得清"的工程公司设计：每条记录都绑定项目、员工、位置和审核状态，墙封上之前拍了什么、票据对着哪次施工，随时查得出来。 Built for companies that must answer "prove it" later: every record is tied to a project, an employee, a location, and a review status — what was shot before the wall was closed, which receipt belongs to which pour.
