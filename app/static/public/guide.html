使用流程How it works

拍照、审核、归档，三步走。Capture, review, file — three steps.

工作区开通后，管理员建项目、加员工、发上传密钥；之后现场和后台各干各的，记录自动汇合。Once your workspace opens, admins set up projects, add employees, and issue upload keys. From there the field and the office each do their part; the records meet in the middle.

从零跑起来Typical rollout

  1. 管理员创建项目和员工，发放上传密钥。Admin creates projects and employees, issues upload keys.
  2. 工人装好 App、录入密钥，到工地就能拍。Crews install the app, enter their key, and start shooting on site.
  3. 照片、视频、票据自动带上项目、工号、GPS 和时间上传。Photos, video, and receipts upload with project, employee, GPS, and time attached.
  4. 经理在后台筛选、批注、审批，挑出能给业主看的照片。Managers filter, annotate, approve, and pick what the client sees.
  5. 需要汇报时，从已审记录一键生成报告。When it's time to report, build one from approved records in a click.
